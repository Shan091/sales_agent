# Otohom Home Controls — Hub and Control Panel

## Otohom Zigbee Hub

The Otohom Zigbee Hub is the central hub for the Zigbee network, enabling communication between Zigbee devices, routers, the cloud and the mobile application. Supports Matter, Wi-Fi and Zigbee.

Salient features:

- Effortless networking: connect multiple sub-devices simultaneously; establish connections even without an active network
- Accidental deletion prevention: automatically resets and reconnects disconnected sub-devices within three minutes
- Multicast and scalability: stable operation of over 80+ high-power devices with quick control
- Cross-protocol and cross-gateway linkage: local linkage across multiple gateways and protocols within a LAN
- Standard Zigbee 3.0 protocol: compliant with Zigbee 3.0 for device compatibility, with customization options for advanced users

## Touch Screen Control Panel

- Manages lights, curtains, ACs and more from one wall-mounted panel
- Multi-functional touchscreen available in 7-inch, 8-inch and 10-inch sizes
- Supports device control, music playback, and Bluetooth pairing with external speakers
- Connectivity: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and Zigbee
- Durable, with a 10-year lifespan
- Customizable to fit individual user needs and preferences
