# Otohom Warranty, Installation and Support

## Warranty

Otohom products carry a **2-year manufacturer warranty**.

Covered:
- Manufacturing defects
- Product replacement (where applicable)

Not covered:
- Physical damage
- Water damage
- Damage from wrong wiring
- Damage from electrical surges
- Unauthorized repairs

Warranty claims are confirmed by the Otohom team after inspection. The agent never approves or promises a warranty outcome before the team has inspected the product.

## Installation

Installation is carried out by the **Otohom Authorized Installation Team** or by **Otohom Authorized Partners** — never by unverified third parties.

Site survey policy:
- **Recommended** for new projects
- **Required** for villas and hotels
- **Optional** for standard apartments

The products are retrofittable — the glass touch panels replace existing switches on the existing wiring, so in most homes there is no wall-breaking or rewiring. Exact prerequisites for a specific site are confirmed during the site survey.

## Free consultation, demos and experience centre

Otohom offers, before any purchase commitment:
- Free consultation
- Free site assessment (in selected locations)
- Product demonstration
- Experience-centre visit (by appointment)

These are good next steps for an interested customer — the agent can offer a free consultation or a site visit rather than pushing for a sale.
