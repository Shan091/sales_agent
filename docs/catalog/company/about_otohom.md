# About Otohom — Company, Services and Support

## Company overview

Otohom was born from Lanware Solutions' innovation hub to meet the growing demand for modern home automation. Founded in 2017 by a team of passionate engineers, it is today a leading player in smart switches and automation solutions for homes and hospitality spaces (hotels, offices, commercial). In-house design expertise and a dedicated production team ensure quality, functionality and design in every product.

Team: 17+ embedded intelligence engineers, 13+ electrical engineering professionals, 12+ mechanical design experts.

## Product series (residential and commercial)

Residential:
- **Grande** — premium glass touch panel collection. Matter-enabled, switch-level power consumption analytics in the Otohom app, diamond-cut aluminum frame, supports regular wiring and 3-phase plus inverter assistance.
- **Eco** — value series with feather-touch glass panels; modular design fits standard box and plate sizes; variants include 4 Switch, Power Switch, Curtain Switch and Dimmer Switch.
- **Hider** — retrofit modules that convert existing manual switches into smart switches without replacing them; dual control (app + physical switch).
- Plus Smart Locks, Security & Sensors ranges.

Commercial / hotel:
- **Grandeur** — hospitality automation panel line: smart door panel (access, alarms, DND / Cleaning / Wait modes), key card holder that activates personalized lighting/climate/entertainment and energy-saving mode on removal, bedside smart panels with lighting/chandelier/night-light/scene/voice control.
- **Magna** — premium Guest Room Management System (GRMS): check-in integration, real-time room status for housekeeping, DND, Welcome Mode, intelligent thermostat, bedside panels with Sleep/Leisure/Read/Film modes, unique sleep-aid system (music, curtains, dimming, white noise, aromatherapy), automatic fragrance dispensing, corridor lighting/climate optimization.

## Solution tiers (residential)

- **Luxury** — custom wired + wireless technologies, Grande series, sensors, security, entertainment, Matter platform for centralized connectivity.
- **Premium** — Grande series for high-end residential properties.
- **Retrofit** — for existing homes, no rewiring (Hider series).
- **Budget** — affordable smart living (Eco and Hider series).

## What Otohom offers

Full suite of smart home services over wireless (Wi-Fi/BLE, Zigbee, Matter) and wired protocols. Core offerings:

- Lighting automation: manage and schedule lights remotely, automated interior and exterior lighting levels
- Shade / blind / curtain / gate automation: one-touch or voice controlled
- Security solutions: digital door locks, cameras, video door phones with real-time alerts and remote monitoring
- Sensors and smart controls: intrusion detection, proximity, gas leakage, water leakage, water level sensors; voice or app control
- Entertainment: scenes automate TV, speakers and other entertainment devices

## Warranty, installation and consultations

- Warranty: 2-year manufacturer warranty, covering manufacturing defects and product replacement (where applicable). Not covered: physical damage, water damage, wrong wiring, electrical surges, unauthorized repairs. Warranty claims are confirmed by the team after inspection.
- Installation: carried out by the Otohom Authorized Installation Team or Authorized Partners. Site survey is recommended for new projects, required for villas and hotels, and optional for standard apartments.
- Before buying, Otohom offers a free consultation, a free site assessment (in selected locations), a product demonstration, and an experience-centre visit by appointment.
