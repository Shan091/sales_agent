# Otohom Curtain, Shade, Blind and Gate Automation

Open or close curtains, shades and blinds with a single touch of a button or a voice command. Different designs and colors available to suit the home. Works with the Otohom app, Alexa and Google Home.

## CURTAIN (curtain automation module)

- Voltage: 100-240V AC, 50/60 Hz
- Connectivity: Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz / 5 GHz, Zigbee
- Dimensions: 108L x 101W x 35H mm
- Wiring: Neutral + Line
- Output: less than 200W (AC forward / reverse motor power)
- Device working temperature: -10°C to 60°C
- Premium glass touch panel, machine-cut aluminum finish
- Retrofittable with existing wiring

## GATE (gate automation module)

- Voltage: 100-240V AC, 50/60 Hz
- Connectivity: Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz / 5 GHz, Zigbee
- Dimensions: 108L x 101W x 35H mm
- Wiring: Neutral + Line
- Device working temperature: -10°C to 60°C
- Controls motorized gates from the Otohom app or by voice command
