# Otohom Smart Switches — Product Catalogue

Otohom residential switch products come in three series:

- **Grande** — the premium glass touch panel collection (panels detailed below). Matter-enabled: works with any Matter-certified smart home ecosystem over a single protocol. Power consumption analytics at the switch level, shown in real time in the Otohom app. Premium diamond-cut aluminum frame in multiple colors. Supports regular wiring and 3-phase; offers inverter assistance. Available in 6 and 8 modular standard switch plate sizes.
- **Eco** — value series with the same feather-touch glass panel experience. Modular design fits standard box and plate sizes. Supports regular wiring, 3-phase, and inverter assistance. Works with Alexa and Google Home. Variants: 4 Switch, Power Switch, Curtain Switch, Dimmer Switch.
- **Hider** — affordable retrofit module that converts existing physical/manual switches into smart switches without replacing them. No rewiring or complex installation; preserves the existing switchboard look. Dual control: Otohom app (remote, schedules, energy monitoring) plus the original physical switch.

All Otohom smart switch panels share these characteristics:

- Premium glass touch panel
- Machine-cut aluminum finish
- Retrofittable: can be easily replaced with existing wiring (no rewiring needed)
- Works with Alexa and Google Home voice assistants
- Connectivity: Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz / 5 GHz, Zigbee
- Voltage: 100-240V AC, 50/60 Hz
- Panel dimensions: 108L x 101W x 35H mm
- Device working temperature: -10°C to 60°C
- Wiring: Neutral + Line required
- Available frame colors: Golden, Black
- Available glass colors: Black, White

The glass touch panel models below (1 SW through 8 SW) belong to the Grande collection.

- Output: 20A (1 gang)
